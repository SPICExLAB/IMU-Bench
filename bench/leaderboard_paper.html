What the audit found

  1. Duplicated participants in OpenWatch. Two groups of subject IDs ({39, 41, 42, 46, 47, 48, 49} and {40, 43}) are the same people, so random subject splits put one person in train and test. IMU-Bench splits over the 43 canonical identities; under that split a re-implemented Mix-Token reaches 0.500 clip F1 on real recordings.
  2. Transductive overlap in activity-recognition pretraining. A common pretraining pool bundles WISDM, UTD-MHAD, PAMAP2, RecoFit, SAMoSA, Shoaib and UT-Watch; nearly every probe validation subject was seen, unlabelled, during pretraining. IMU-Bench therefore leaves WISDM, UTD-MHAD, PAMAP2, RecoFit, SAMoSA and UT-Watch out of its evaluation set.
  3. Inconsistent sensor units across sources. EatingSpeed gyroscope data are stored in degrees per second while the other sources use radians per second. The encoders on this page are unaffected: their input is z-scored per window and channel, which removes any constant scale. Physical-units pipelines must convert.

How much pretraining data a wrist IMU needs

With all 1,000 motion modes available, 0.1 % of a 1.96-million-window pool (1,959 windows) matches the full pool on reconstruction loss and on the kinematic probes; 0.5 % of the pool already covers 99.7 % of its motion modes.
